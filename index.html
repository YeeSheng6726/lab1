Frequently Asked Questions

Is this class suitable for beginners?

Absolutely! This class is designed for all experience levels. We start with the fundamentals and build up to more advanced concepts. Beginners will gain a solid foundation, while experienced investors will learn new strategies and techniques.

Will I need any special software or accounts?

No special software is required. We'll use free online tools and resources. You don't need any investment accounts to participate - this is an educational workshop focused on teaching you the strategies and principles.

How do I claim this training through HRDC?

This workshop is HRDC claimable for Malaysian companies. After completing your registration, we'll provide an official quotation and detailed course curriculum. Your company's HR team can then submit the training grant application through the e-TRiS portal. Please ensure the application is submitted at least 24 hours before the workshop begins. Our team will guide you through the entire claims process to ensure everything is properly documented.
